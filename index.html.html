KALSTONE STUDIO is an independent creative studio built around a simple idea: great ideas deserve great visual expression.

Based in Port Moresby, we draw inspiration from the rich, diverse cultural heritage of Papua New Guinea, blending it with modern, minimalist design principles to create brands that stand out on a global stage.

We help businesses, entrepreneurs and brands transform their ideas into memorable identities, engaging content and powerful visual experiences. Whether you are a local startup or an international enterprise, our focus remains the same: delivering premium, impactful creative work.
